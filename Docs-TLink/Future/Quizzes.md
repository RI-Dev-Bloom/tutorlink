# Quizzes

**In short:** tutors write quizzes, and students play them live in a session or in their own time.

## Ideas
- **Self-paced:** open for a set period, students do it whenever, and see their score.
- **Live / timed:** the tutor starts it in the session, everyone answers on their phone at the same time, with a timer and a leaderboard (Kahoot-style).
- The tutor announces a quiz and students get notified.
- The tutor sees which questions most people got wrong.

## Fits the free tier?
Yes. Live quizzes use Supabase Realtime, which allows about 200 people connected at once, plenty for a tutorial room.
