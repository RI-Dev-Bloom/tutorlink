# Student module dashboard

**In short:** for each module a student picked: who the tutor is, when the next session is, resources and activities.

## Ideas
- A card per module with the tutor's name and the next session (time and venue).
- Resources the tutor shares (links and files).
- Activities: [[Quizzes]] and, later, [[Attendance]].
- [[Announcements]] at the top.

## Why
It gives students a reason to open the app every week, and gets more people to sessions.
