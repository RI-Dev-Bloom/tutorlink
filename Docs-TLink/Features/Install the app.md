# Install the app

**In short:** a short guide that helps people add TutorLink to their home screen.

## What it does
- Android (Chrome): an **Install app** button, or if the phone doesn't offer one, **Show me how** with three steps.
- iPhone (Safari): **Show me how** opens three steps: tap Share, tap Add to Home Screen, open it from the home screen.
- Once installed, it opens full screen like a normal app and can receive notifications.

## Why it's here
We're not in the App Store (that costs money). On iPhone, web notifications **only** work once the app has been added to the home screen. Without this step, iPhone users would never get notified.

## Rules
- Shown as step 1 of the **Get set up** card on the home screen until it's done or closed, and always available in **Profile → Device**.
- Never shown once the app is installed, and not shown on computers.
