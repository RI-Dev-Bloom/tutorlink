# Tutor management

**In short:** for each tutor, admin can see and handle their sessions, timetable, limit and pay on one page.

## What it does
- The **Tutors** tab lists every tutor with their modules, "X of Y sessions" used, money owed, and a **to confirm** badge. Search by name, student number or module.
- Tapping a tutor opens their page:
  - **Session limit:** "9 of 24 sessions used" with a bar. Tap **Change** to give this tutor their own limit, or put them back on the semester default. See [[Session quota]].
  - **Needs confirming:** sessions that have happened. **Confirm** each one after checking the register, or tap **Didn't happen** if the tutor didn't run it (it then stops counting).
  - **Pay:** what's owed. **Mark N as paid** once the money has actually gone out. Paid sessions are locked.
  - **Weekly slots:** add one (see [[Schedule]]) or **Stop** one from a date, which cancels the weeks still to come.
  - **Coming up:** cancel a single week (e.g. a public holiday) and restore it if plans change.
  - **Add a one-off session** for a session outside the timetable. It's confirmed straight away.
  - **Registers** the tutor uploaded, tap to open.
  - A history of confirmed and paid sessions.
- The **Overview** tab shows what needs doing: applications waiting, sessions to confirm, money owed, and which tutors have sessions waiting.

## Why it's here
Confirming and paying is the biggest recurring admin job, and it needs to work while walking between offices.
