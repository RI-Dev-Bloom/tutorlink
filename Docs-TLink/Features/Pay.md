# Pay

**In short:** shows the tutor what they're owed. The money itself is paid outside the app.

## What it does
- A big figure for **approved** (confirmed but not yet paid).
- What's **pending**, the **rate**, and the projected total.
- One line for each confirmed session.
- **Export** downloads a CSV statement.

## Why it's here
"Have I been paid?" is the second question every tutor has. Showing pending money separately heads off arguments before they start.

## Rules
- The app tracks money but doesn't pay anyone. Rosebank pays as usual.
- The rate comes from [[Settings]] (R150 per session to start).
