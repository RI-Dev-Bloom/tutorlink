# Approve and decline

**In short:** admin picks a response (a template or their own words), then approves or declines.

## What it does
- Pick one of the ready-made responses from [[Settings]], or write your own. You can't decide without a message.
- **Approve:** the student becomes a tutor for those modules, gets a welcome message, and gets a push notification.
- **Decline:** the student sees the response on their application.

## Why it's here
Nobody gets a silent decline, and templates keep the wording consistent and kind. Approval and notification happen in the same moment.
