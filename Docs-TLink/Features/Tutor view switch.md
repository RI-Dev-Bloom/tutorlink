# Tutor view switch

**In short:** a tutor uses the same login as a student, and switches between the Student and Tutor views.

## What it does
A switch in the header flips between the student screens and the tutor screens. The app remembers the last one you used.

## Why it's here
A tutor is still a student in their other modules. Two accounts would mean two logins, two sets of notifications, and confusion.
