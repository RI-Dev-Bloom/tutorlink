# App feel

**In short:** the small details that make it feel like a real app rather than a website.

- **Screens slide** in from the right, and back reverses it.
- **Swipe from the left edge** to go back. Android's back button works too.
- **Bottom tab bar** within thumb reach.
- **Panels slide up from the bottom** instead of pop-up boxes, and you drag them down to close.
- **Buttons press in** slightly when tapped.
- **Haptics:** small vibrations on Android. iPhone doesn't allow this for web apps.
- **Toasts:** a small bar confirms what just happened.
- **Safe areas:** nothing hides under the notch.
- **Reduce Motion** is respected.
- **16px inputs**, so iPhone doesn't zoom in when you tap a field.

All of this is already in the demo and carries straight over. The design is navy, gold and cream with serif headings, matching the Rosebank look.
