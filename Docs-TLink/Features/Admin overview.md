# Admin overview

**In short:** admin's home screen: where the semester is, what needs doing, the money, and whether every high-risk module is covered.

## What it shows
1. **The semester band** (navy): the week we're in (e.g. "14, week of 20"), a timeline from start to end, days left, and the programme's reach: **sessions run**, **students reached** and the **average per session**.
2. **To do:** only what needs admin, with a count. Applications waiting (and how long the oldest has waited), each tutor with sessions to confirm, and tutors close to their session limit. When there's nothing: "All caught up".
3. **Pay this semester:** the payout building up for the semester end: confirmed so far, waiting to be confirmed, still scheduled, and what it could reach. Tap it for the pay sheet. See [[Pay]].
4. **Week by week:** a bar per week of the semester showing sessions run. This week is gold. Tap a bar for that week's sessions and students.
5. **High-risk modules:** each one with how many tutors it has and sessions run. A module with no tutor is flagged red, because those students have nobody.

## Why it's here
Admin's job is mostly a queue, so the to-do list is near the top. The rest answers the questions management will ask: is it reaching students, what will it cost, and are the modules that need help covered?
