# Upload a register

**In short:** the tutor photographs the signed attendance sheet or attaches a PDF, against a module.

## What it does
On the **Sessions** tab, tap **Upload**. Pick the session it's for (the most recent one without a register is chosen for you), then take a photo or pick a file. It's saved privately, and admin sees it on the tutor's page, with a "register ✓" next to that session in their confirm list. Uploaded the wrong file? Tap the ✕ to remove it.

## Why it's here
It's the proof admin checks before confirming a session. The phone camera makes this a 10-second job.

## Rules
- Photos are shrunk on the phone before uploading, to save space on the free tier.
- Only that tutor and admin can see it.
- A register can only be attached to the tutor's own session, for a module they tutor.
