# Upload a register

**In short:** the tutor photographs the signed attendance sheet or attaches a PDF, against a module.

## What it does
Choose a module, then take a photo or pick a file. It's saved privately, and admin sees it on the tutor's page.

## Why it's here
It's the proof admin checks before confirming a session. The phone camera makes this a 10-second job.

## Rules
- Photos are shrunk on the phone before uploading, to save space on the free tier.
- Only that tutor and admin can see it.
