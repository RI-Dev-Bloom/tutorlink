# Module picker

**In short:** students pick their qualification and year, and that year's modules **for this semester** are added for them. They can tick or untick any after that.

## What it does
- On first sign-in, students search for their qualification (grouped by faculty), then choose **Year 1, 2 or 3**. They see the modules that will be added before they save.
- Those modules are added straight away. Electives (like a home language) aren't added: students tick their own.
- The **Modules** tab shows the qualification and year at the top, with **Change**. Changing them adds the new year's modules and keeps the old ones.
- **Change my modules** shows the qualification's modules by year, with the student's year marked. Tap to add or remove, saved straight away.
- The search finds any module on the official list, for students repeating or taking one from another qualification.

## Rules
- Students can't add modules any more. The list is Rosebank's official one, and only admin can add to it (see [[Manage modules]]).
- If a module is missing, students ask admin.
- The year comes from the module code: the second digit is the year (PROG**5**121 is year 1, HCIN**6**222 is year 2). One-year qualifications are all year 1.
- **The semester comes from the last digit** (25 Oct): ...1 (or ...3) is semester 1, ...2 (or ...4) semester 2, ...0 or ...9 the whole year. PROG512**1** Programming 1A is semester 1, PROG611**2** Programming 1B is semester 2, XISD531**9** Work Integrated Learning runs all year. Which semester we're in comes from the semester dates in Settings (Jan–Jun start is 1, Jul–Dec is 2).
- The Modules tab shows **this semester's** modules; any others they have show under "Not this semester". The picker lists this semester's first and tags the rest "Other semester".
