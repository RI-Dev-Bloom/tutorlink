# Unread and delivered

**In short:** a gold dot means unread, and the sender sees "delivered" until it's opened.

## What it does
- **Gold dot:** unread. **Hollow ring:** read. **Solid navy:** you sent it.
- A count on the mail icon and the Messages tab goes down as you open messages.
- The sender sees **Delivered** until the other person opens it.

## Why it's here
Gold is used in only a few places, so a gold dot grabs your eye. And if you told a tutor the venue moved, you want to know they've seen it.
